mcmetal no-DH test pack (MIT, ours). Stands in for SEUS PTGI in tests: a deferred pack that paints its own sky in a
composite pass wherever nothing was drawn (depth 1.0) and has no dh_* programs, so Iris reports "No DH shader found"
and Distant Horizons' far terrain is covered by the pack. The lavender-to-blue sky makes it easy to see that
mcmetal's DH composite (MCMETAL_DH_COMPOSITE) fades the LODs into the pack's own sky colour.
